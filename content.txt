B - Business overview

	•	Describe your business and business model.
The ProPower Engineering & Contracting LLC is an engineering and contracting company specializing in electrical power transmission and distribution solutions for medium- and low-voltage networks. We provide end-to-end electrical solutions including electrical installation, power cabling, substations and transformers, protection and control, retrofit and system upgradation, testing and commissioning, and operation and maintenance.

Our portfolio also covers data center critical power, industrial automation and instrumentation, ELV and communication systems, fire and life safety, residential and commercial electrical solutions, and infrastructure-related services.

Our business model is project-based and solution-oriented, serving utility, industrial, commercial, infrastructure and data center clients. We combine engineering, project execution, testing and commissioning, maintenance services and OEM-supported solutions to deliver reliable and cost-effective solutions tailored to individual project requirements.

	•	What distinguishes your company from your competitors? Please provide a detailed explanation.
Pro Power differentiates itself through its combination of technical expertise, integrated electrical engineering capabilities and project execution experience across utility, industrial, commercial and infrastructure sectors.

Key differentiators include:

1. Integrated Electrical & Power Capabilities We provide a broad range of solutions covering LV/MV electrical systems, power transmission and distribution, substations and transformers, protection and control, power cabling, electrical installations and related infrastructure.
2. End-to-End Project Support Our capabilities extend beyond installation to include system upgradation, modification, retrofit, testing and commissioning, operation and maintenance, allowing us to support clients throughout the project lifecycle.
3. Utility & Critical Infrastructure Experience Our project references include work associated with SEWA, DEWA and EtihadWE, as well as critical facilities such as airports, district cooling, data centers and major commercial and industrial clients.
4. In-House Testing & Commissioning Capability We maintain a range of professional testing equipment, including primary and secondary injection kits, circuit breaker analyzers, insulation resistance testers, high-potential testing equipment and other electrical testing instruments. This supports our ability to undertake electrical testing and commissioning activities effectively.
5. Multi-Disciplinary Solutions In addition to core electrical and power services, our portfolio covers data center solutions, industrial automation and instrumentation, ELV and communication systems, fire and life safety, retrofit and maintenance, and infrastructure solutions.
6. Quality, Environmental & Safety Commitment Our management systems are aligned with ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018, demonstrating our focus on quality management, environmental responsibility and occupational health and safety.
7. Solution-Oriented & Client-Focused Approach We focus on providing reliable, technically appropriate and cost-effective solutions tailored to the specific requirements of each project and client.
