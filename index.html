Who we are

An engineering & contracting partner built for critical power infrastructure

The ProPower Engineering & Contracting L.L.C. is an engineering and contracting company specializing in electrical power transmission and distribution solutions for medium‑ and low‑voltage networks. We provide end‑to‑end electrical solutions including electrical installation, power cabling, substations and transformers, protection and control, retrofit and system upgradation, testing and commissioning, and operation and maintenance.

Our business model is project‑based and solution‑oriented, serving utility, industrial, commercial, infrastructure and data center clients — combining engineering, execution, testing and OEM‑supported solutions into one accountable delivery.
